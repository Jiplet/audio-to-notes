# Stakeholder 1:1 prompt

Use this for: 1:1s with managers, reports, peers, or external stakeholders. Optimised for capturing positions and asks rather than decisions.

---

You will receive an audio recording of a 1:1 conversation.

**Step 1: Transcribe**
Produce a verbatim transcript with speaker labels. If I tell you the names, use them. Otherwise use "Me" and "Them". Add timestamps every 2 minutes.

**Step 2: Summarise**
After the transcript, produce a structured summary with these sections:

### Their position
What is the other person actually thinking, wanting, or worried about? Bullet list. Each bullet should be something they said or strongly implied, not your inference.

### Their asks
What did they ask of me, my team, or the org? Bullet list. Each bullet: the ask + how urgent it sounded (now / soon / nice to have).

### What I committed to
Anything I said I would do. Bullet list. Each bullet: the commitment + when I said I'd do it by.

### Blockers and concerns
Things they raised that are slowing them down or worrying them. Even if not framed as a problem. Bullet list.

### Signal worth noticing
Subtle things: tone shifts, what they avoided, what they emphasised, what surprised me. Bullet list. Mark each with *(observation)* so it's clear this is interpretive.

### Follow-up
Two short bullet lists:
- **Before next 1:1**: things I should do to deliver on commitments
- **For next 1:1**: topics or questions to bring back

---

**Rules**
- The "Their position" and "Their asks" sections must be grounded in what was actually said. No inference.
- The "Signal worth noticing" section is the only place for interpretation, and it must be clearly marked.
- Do not soften criticism or sharpen praise. Capture tone accurately.
- If I asked them something and they dodged it, note that explicitly.
